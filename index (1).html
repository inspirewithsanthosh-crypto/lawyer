About the Firm

Counsel that treats your matter like it's the only one we have.

Founded in 2008, Sterling & Voss is a boutique firm built around direct partner involvement — every client works with an attorney, not a case manager. We take on a limited number of matters at a time so nothing gets handled at arm's length.

Our approach favors clear counsel over billable hours: we tell clients plainly what a matter will cost, how long it will take, and what the realistic outcomes are.

  • Founded 2008, three-attorney partnership
  • Bar admissions New York, New Jersey, Connecticut
  • Focus areas Corporate, litigation, family, real estate, estates
